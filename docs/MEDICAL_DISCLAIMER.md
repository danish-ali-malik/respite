# Medical disclaimer

**Respite is a wellness habit tool, not a medical device, diagnosis, or treatment.**

## What Respite is for

Respite helps you practice regular short visual breaks inspired by the commonly cited **20-20-20 rule**:

> Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet (6 metres) away for at least 20 seconds.

Many eye-care organizations, including the American Academy of Ophthalmology, recommend regular breaks to ease temporary discomfort associated with prolonged screen use (often called digital eye strain).

## What Respite does not claim

Respite does **not** claim to:

- Prevent keratoconus or any eye disease
- Cure or treat eye conditions
- Prevent permanent vision deterioration
- Improve refractive error (nearsightedness, farsightedness, astigmatism)
- Guarantee eye health outcomes
- Replace professional eye examinations or care

## Evidence note

Research on the exact 20-20-20 schedule is mixed. Some studies report reduced digital-eye-strain *symptoms* with regular breaks; other controlled studies find little short-term effect from 20-second scheduled pauses alone. Breaks may help comfort for some people and less for others. Configurable intervals are provided so you can adapt the habit to what feels sustainable.

If you have eye pain, sudden vision changes, or a diagnosed condition, consult a qualified eye-care professional.
