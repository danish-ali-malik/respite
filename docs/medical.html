Medical disclaimer

Respite is a wellness habit tool, not a medical device, diagnosis, or treatment.

Respite helps you practice regular short visual breaks inspired by the 20-20-20 rule. Many eye-care organizations recommend regular breaks to ease temporary discomfort associated with prolonged screen use.

Respite does not claim to:

  • Prevent keratoconus or any eye disease
  • Cure or treat eye conditions
  • Prevent permanent vision deterioration
  • Improve refractive error
  • Guarantee eye health outcomes
  • Replace professional eye examinations or care

If you have eye pain, sudden vision changes, or a diagnosed condition, consult a qualified eye-care professional.
